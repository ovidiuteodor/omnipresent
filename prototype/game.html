About

Signalis is a classic survival horror game developed by rose-engine, set in a dystopian, retro-futuristic world heavily inspired by Cold War aesthetics, cosmic horror, and anime. Players control Elster, a technician Replika searching a desolate subterranean facility for her lost human partner and her own fragmented memories, blending nostalgic PS1-era visuals, scarce resource management, and intricate puzzles with a haunting, surrealist story.
